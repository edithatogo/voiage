# Specification: Q08 Producer & Public-Health Workflows

Covers roadmap packages **V08** (realistic data & risk-prediction workflows), **F06** (statistical & economic integration), **F07** (public-health & distributional workflows), and **F08** (cross-library producer workflows).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Qualify installed `innovate` / `paignia` / `tobac` producer exports on `voiage.simulation-outcome.v1` and `NormalizedInputBundle` without importing producer simulation engines into `voiage` core.
2. Fail closed on wrong units, weights, timebases, missing providers, and incompatible export versions.
3. Add realistic-data and risk-prediction / public-health / distributional reproduction workflows (`V08`, `F07`) with provenance, rights, and synthetic-vs-empirical disclosure.
