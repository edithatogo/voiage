# Specification: R01–R03 Advanced Research Extensions

Covers roadmap packages **V09** (Bayesian experimental design & acquisition), **V11** (strategic information design), and **F03** (advanced design & optimization), gated on `Q05`–`Q08`.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. **R01 (`V07`, `V09`, `F03`):** Decision-aware simulation allocation/stopping passes sequential error-control and time-to-decision-accuracy benchmarks vs. fixed-budget baselines.
2. **R02 (`V06`, `V10`, `F06`):** Joint research–implementation–capacity optimization matches exact small cases and held-out scenarios vs. sequential/uptake-multiplier baselines.
3. **R03 (`V08`, `V10`, `V11`, `F06`):** Transport-bias and strategic/perspective information design modules pass explicit feasibility, gaming/adversarial, and external-validity checks before promotion out of `experimental`.
