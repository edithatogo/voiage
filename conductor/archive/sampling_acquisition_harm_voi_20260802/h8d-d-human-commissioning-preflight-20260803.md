# H8-D-D human commissioning preflight

Native child [#870](https://github.com/edithatogo/voiage/issues/870) turns the
remaining H8-D prerequisites into a deterministic, human-usable handoff without
claiming that any prerequisite has been satisfied.

## Candidate-context decision

The preflight leaves the accountable selection unset and presents three
mutually explicit paths:

1. **Recommended:** seek independent review of the proposed exclusion of a
   universal automatic scalar or study-authorizing kernel, while preserving
   narrower candidate-bound research.
2. Define a bounded non-authorizing candidate for one declared domain,
   jurisdiction, population, sampling action, comparator and affected-party
   set, accepting the additional source, estimator, domain and ethics burden.
3. Defer scientific disposition and retain unsupported research scoping.

A generic continuation instruction is not the content-bound candidate choice.
No option is selected by this repository preparation.

## Commissioning inputs

The canonical preflight binds the raw SHA-256 bytes of the remediation delta,
reviewer intake, source intake and automated source observations. It retains
all nineteen pending findings, the five source-review prerequisite findings,
five H8-D roles and the two distinct H8-G confirmation roles. Every role remains
unassigned and ineligible.

The handoff explains the role rubrics and routes future outputs to the existing
reviewer-attestation, role-report, finding-disposition and scientific-approval
schemas. It prohibits credentials, personal contact details and raw signatures
in the public repository; only minimum consented evidence and authenticated
receipt identifiers may be recorded.

## Fail-closed result

Commissioning status remains `blocked_prerequisites`. Candidate selection,
independent retrieval, source rights, jurisdictional applicability, eligible
reviewer assignment, replacement-packet freeze and all finding dispositions are
absent. Consequently source authority, reviewer eligibility, scientific
disposition, H8-D through H8-H, runtime, real-study, release and publication
authority all remain false.

Repository validation and a green merged PR may complete only H8-D-D
preparation. Qualified humans and accountable source evidence remain external
inputs to the still-open parent #853.
