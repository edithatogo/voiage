The intended diffusion repository is `innovate`, following the recorded ownership
map. Do not create a separate `Unnovate` repository from the conversational spelling.
Existing user-owned Hub collections may supply governed evidence after inspection,
but are not automatically mathematical VOI benchmarks. No sibling or Hub writes are
included here. Each later producer change needs its own scoped PR and qualification.

Share draw IDs, joint-sample/weight conventions, units, perspective, horizon, RNG
identity, diagnostics and provenance. Do not force identical APIs, internal classes,
release cadence or dependency graphs. Scientific semantics, interchange, API and ABI
are four separate commitments. Same-author repositories demonstrate reuse, not
independent external adoption.

## Research questions and go/no-go evidence

| Packet | Question | Baselines and decisive evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01 | Can computation be allocated/stopped by its potential to change the selected research decision? | Fixed-budget nested/surrogate methods and relevant sequential/multilevel approaches; justified sequential error control, wrong-decision probability and time-to-prespecified decision accuracy |
| R02 | When does jointly choosing research, implementation and capacity change the preferred investment? | Research-only ENBS, independent uptake multipliers and sequential allocation; exact finite cases, uncertainty/dependence preservation, held-out scenarios and realised population timing |
| R03 | How do uncertain bias, transferability, equity or stakeholder perspectives change the preferred evidence source? | Conventional trial/observational comparisons and explicit structural models; identification assumptions, misspecification sensitivity, reproducible decision changes |

Begin each with a current primary-source novelty review and a bounded feasibility
protocol. A combination of familiar components is not sufficient novelty. Record
negative/inconclusive results and separate computational advantage from scientific
identification. These investigations do not automatically become stable features.

## Publication route

The existing release/venue track remains the submission authority. Its current
sequence is **pyOpenSci first, then the eligible JOSS partner route, with rOpenSci
separately and arXiv deferred**. This refines the previous recommendation to target
JOSS; it does not silently reverse an existing maintainer decision.

| Output | Preferred destination | Entry evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Initial software paper | JOSS via the existing pyOpenSci route when eligible | Released, installable declared scope; sustained public development, demonstrated research use, honest maturity matrix and reproducible examples |
| Main comparative evaluation | Medical Decision Making | Prespecified independent references, fair baselines, errors and decision consequences, failed cases and full reproduction bundle |
| New statistical estimator/stopping method | Statistics in Medicine; consider SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification for general numerical work | Verified novelty, precise assumptions and suitable theory/benchmarks; refresh venue fit before selecting |
| Joint implementation/equity/perspective method | Value in Health | Substantive HEOR contribution, realistic application and sensitivity to policy/implementation assumptions |
| Broad mature statistical-software exposition | Journal of Statistical Software as a distinct alternative/later contribution | Substantial software contribution and complete reproducibility; not a duplicate JOSS manuscript or a substitute venue for new methodology |

Venue choices are recommendations, not acceptance predictions. The first software
paper need not wait for every experimental method. Do not fragment every small
feature into a separate paper. Disclose overlapping outputs and never submit the
same manuscript to two journals simultaneously. Preserve `paper/main.tex` as the
canonical preprint and the existing JOSS adaptation; load manuscript metadata,
readiness and skills before editing either.

JOSS policies checked in this planning pass permit disclosed AI assistance but
require human responsibility and prohibit AI-generated author–editor/reviewer
conversation except translation. Do not generate or send those exchanges. Existing
survey, human-text, authorship, licence, upload and editorial gates stay open.
Recheck current policies immediately before submission; no author attestation,
scientific acceptance, journal action or data publication occurs here.

## Deliverables

Q09 assembles: a preregistered or timestamped confirmatory protocol; pinned reference
corpus; accuracy/decision-quality and matched-accuracy performance reports; an
installed cross-language reproduction; one source-to-decision application; rights
and AI-use disclosures; and the existing venue-specific checklists. Arrange an
independent reproduction and record what actually occurred. An agent reviewer is
not an external human adopter or a journal peer reviewer.
