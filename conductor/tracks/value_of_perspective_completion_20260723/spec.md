## Overview

Owning issue: [#317](https://github.com/edithatogo/voiage/issues/317). Parent programme issue [#313](https://github.com/edithatogo/voiage/issues/313).

## Requirements

1. Define directional and pairwise regret, perspective acceptability, switch regions, and perfect/partial/sample perspective-information estimands.
2. Support joint parameter-perspective uncertainty, consensus, maximin, minimax-regret, Pareto and raw-outcome transformations.
3. Preserve directional asymmetry, reference perspective, weights, comparability and tie policy in the shared VOP contract.
4. Require C01/C02 conformance, analytical switch points, decomposition identities and rights-cleared multi-perspective examples.
