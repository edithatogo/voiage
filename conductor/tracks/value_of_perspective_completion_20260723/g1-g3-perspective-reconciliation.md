## Frozen VOP contract

The contract keeps directional and pairwise regret, acceptability, switch
regions, perfect/partial/sample information, reference perspective, weights,
consensus, maximin, minimax-regret and Pareto transformations distinct. It
preserves directional asymmetry, explicit tie policy and raw-outcome versus
transformed-value semantics. Perspective is not preference, equity,
heterogeneity or ordinary EVPI.

Rust remains the stable numerical authority; Python is the current public
orchestration surface. R, Julia and Mojo are unsupported for this family until
installed shared fixtures and binding-specific contracts exist. Rights-cleared
multi-perspective examples, scientific review, release and publication remain
separate gates.
