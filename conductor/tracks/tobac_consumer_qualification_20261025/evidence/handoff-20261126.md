## 3. Rollback Procedure

If a downstream `tobac` workflow observes an incompatible change or regression:

1. **Pin back to the prior qualified `voiage==3.1.0` wheel** using its verified PyPI SHA-256 (`ca1a625ea2e0f6c63ffd306f19cb62c1c69242643164553d5ca633e94146eb5b` for `macosx_11_0_arm64`) in `tobac`'s `engineering/voiage-published-requirements.txt` and lockfiles.
2. **Preserve fail-closed checks:** Do NOT bypass `expected_version` checks, relax `rtol`/`atol` tolerances (`1e-10`), weaken `AnalysisSpec`/`NormalizedInputBundle` digest validation, or silently fall back to an alternative backend when `allow_fallback=False`.
3. **Experimental credal/censored APIs:** `voiage.experimental.{credal_decisions, paired_draw_bundle, censored_sequential_evsi}` sit on post-`3.1.0` `main` and remain explicitly labelled experimental until promoted in a signed release.
