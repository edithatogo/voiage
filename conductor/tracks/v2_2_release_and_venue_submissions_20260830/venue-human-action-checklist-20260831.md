## R14: contact capacity

at another venue. Human-written review communication is expected, with limited

## R17: retained JOSS prerequisites

The [current JOSS author guidance](https://joss.readthedocs.io/en/latest/submitting.html)
requires demonstrated research use, current AI disclosure and human review,
and author responsibility. It treats broader community engagement as a strong
positive signal in pre-review, and permits preprints before, during or after
review. JOSS editor/reviewer conversations may use AI only for translation.
The project's non-author-engagement and arXiv-before-JOSS requirements remain
author-selected prerequisites; they must not be misreported as universal JOSS
pre-review rules or silently removed.

- Preserve `paper/joss-developer-research-use.json`: the recorded human use
  concerns v2.0.0. `paper/research-use/v2.2.0/` is an automated replay, not new
  human research use or adoption. Confirm current claims with the author.
- Obtain genuine attributable non-author engagement. Live
  [issue #471](https://github.com/edithatogo/voiage/issues/471) remains open with
  only two author comments. Its latest invitation still names v2.0.0; a future
  human-written invitation should make the intended release explicit. Bots,
  subagents and same-author repositories cannot satisfy this prerequisite.
- Retain pyOpenSci acceptance and exact accepted revision, and establish JOSS
  partner eligibility before initiating the selected route.
- Review current paper claims and citations, authorship, affiliations, ORCID,
  funding, competing interests, and the complete current AI disclosure. Existing
  historical declarations are evidence only for their recorded scope and date.
- Complete the author-selected arXiv sequence. The July observation of draft
  `7870358`, with an August 9 expiry, is historical and is not a current account
  check or permanent identifier. The author must choose category, licence and
  endorsement path, review the final source package/PDF, and perform or
  explicitly authorize authenticated action. Preserve `paper/main.tex` as the
  canonical preprint source. Do not infer submission or announcement.
- Before JOSS acceptance, retain a DOI-bearing archive for the reviewed
  version. Public v2.2.0 release evidence and the historical v2.0.0 Software
  Heritage snapshot do not satisfy that reviewed-version DOI record.
