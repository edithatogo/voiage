## Synthesized recommendation

Select `generic_cross_domain_sampling_acquisition_harm_kernel` as the exact
review target with proposed disposition `reviewed_exclusion`. Record the
scientific disposition as pending, expose no runtime symbol, and proceed to
H8-C–H8-H. The future packet must bind the exact Git commit and tree, source
and artifact digests, scope, limitations, reports, findings, disagreements,
dispositions and verified human receipts.

Consideration of a narrow executable candidate may reopen only after a named
domain, jurisdiction, population, sampling action and comparator, affected
parties, applicable authority profile, identified harm law and data, reviewed
risk ordering and component ledger, estimator assurance, independent reports
and two named human confirmations exist. Runtime engineering and real-study
authorization remain later, separate gates.
