# Specification: Q06 Research Design & Portfolio Decisions

Covers roadmap packages **V06** (economic study-design optimization), **V10** (selective frontier extensions: structural/equity/behavioral/adaptive), and **F06** (statistical & economic integration).

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Verify ENBS (`50 * 100 - 6000 = -1000`) under explicit single-period undiscounted fixtures including the no-study option.
2. Enumerate small design and portfolio optima independently (ties, binding budgets, shared outcomes, overlapping evidence); reject unsupported dependence rather than summing incompatible EVSI values.
3. Enforce population timing, discounting, recruitment delay, implementation, and capacity constraints without unit mixing or double counting.
4. Score selected-design regret alongside scalar error and document exact/approximate/unsupported algorithm status.
