## Numerical assessment

Independent calculations agreed with the reported values:

- theoretical EVPI: 652.182; fixed-sample estimate: 644.154;
- theoretical health-gain EVPPI: 598.413; estimate: 589.666;
- theoretical programme-cost EVPPI: 259.312; estimate: 249.595;
- EVSI at total sample size 200: 124.179;
- immediate ENBS sign change: between 100 and 200 evaluated participants;
- delayed 60% realisation sign change: between 800 and 1,200.

The normal--normal EVSI model, population scaling, study-cost subtraction,
discounting, delay, implementation assumption, and synthetic-data boundaries
were scientifically coherent.

## Manuscript findings

- Define VOI as expected improvement in decision value, not the intrinsic value
  of certainty.
- Distinguish EVPI/EVPPI simulated inputs, EVSI study-model inputs, and ENBS
  costs.
- Frame cross-domain reach as representational rather than validated use.
- Scope stable-method parity to interfaces that expose the method.
- Name ordinary least squares for the deliberately linear EVPPI illustration.
- Label all intervals as finite-simulation Monte Carlo intervals.
- Strengthen the build-versus-contribute rationale.
