# Sentence-level scientific-editor review

## Role and basis

This role read every sentence of the initial Round-10 `paper.md`, alongside the
JOSS contract, claim ledger, release and reproduction evidence, and the panel
rubric. It evaluated precision, grammar, scope, terminology, citation
placement, and redundancy rather than acting as an automated grammar checker.

## Initial assessment

Score: **927/1000**, capped by stale source-bound assurance, pending
source-by-source citation confirmation, and the unsupported final human
AI-review attestation. The article was otherwise structurally complete and
scientifically restrained.

## Sentence-level changes accepted

- Replaced compressed definitions in the summary with plain-language decision
  meanings for the four VOI measures.
- Made the build-versus-contribute rationale conditional: an environment-based
  interoperability layer would add that environment as a dependency or require
  a compatibility layer; neither outcome is asserted as inevitable.
- Replaced the imprecise phrase about a distribution “generating expected net
  benefit” with a distinction between the generating expectation and finite
  sample mean.
- Recast hosted-platform wording as workflow configuration pending current
  hosted evidence.
- Simplified bootstrap, regression, study-value, caption, and provenance prose
  while preserving the numerical values and their stated assumptions.

## Sentence inventory conclusion

No retained sentence was judged promotional or to imply independent adoption.
The following claims remain intentionally unresolved because they require human
or external evidence: final citation verification, final AI-review attestation,
completed developer research use, and non-author engagement.

## Re-review criteria

The next pass must inspect the exact revised source sentence by sentence,
including all captions, metadata, declarations, and references. It should
record no unresolved prose defect and distinguish a prose pass from the pending
human and external gates.
