# Round 6 JOSS article-contract review

**Assessment point:** pre-contract manuscript, before the 1,627-word revision.

This is an AI-assisted internal review. It is not a JOSS editorial decision,
acceptance assessment, or authorisation to submit.

## Overall finding

The manuscript was blocked against a fail-closed contract derived from the
current JOSS submission guide, paper format, review criteria, review
checklist, policies, and editorial guide. The review separated defects in the
article from repository evidence and external eligibility gates.

The review also found that an arXiv identifier is not a JOSS requirement.
Obtaining the permanent identifier before JOSS submission is retained only as
the author's requested project sequence.

## Blocking findings at the assessment point

- The deterministic body count was 1,705 words. The repository's stricter
  1,600 ±2% target permits 1,568 through 1,632 words.
- The article had no machine-readable section contract, claim ledger, or
  source-bound editorial-assurance record.
- The exact reviewed revision had no matching immutable release or archive.
- Attributable non-author use, community engagement, or collaborative input
  had not been documented for this single-author project.
- Human confirmation remained necessary for the final citation-source check
  and the complete AI-output review and validation attestation.

## Required contract

The reviewer required:

1. exact metadata, heading order, and substantive section rules;
2. an exact 1,600-word target with a 1,568–1,632 acceptance band;
3. claim-to-evidence and citation-to-record reconciliation;
4. deterministic numerical regeneration and an official Open Journals build;
5. separate manuscript, repository, human, project-sequence, and external
   gates;
6. a complete AI-use disclosure covering systems, known versions, locations,
   scope, human primary decisions, review, modification, validation, and
   accountability; and
7. machine states that never claim JOSS eligibility, submission, review,
   acceptance, or publication without authoritative evidence.

## Machine and human boundary

Automation can check structure, word count, citations, hashes, tests, and
build outputs. It cannot decide JOSS scope or significance, authenticate
community engagement, certify the truth of author declarations, judge whether
comparisons are complete and fair, or substitute for JOSS editors and
reviewers.

## Disposition

The findings were accepted. The current implementation is recorded in
`paper/joss-article-contract.json`,
`paper/joss-readiness-manifest.json`,
`paper/joss-claim-evidence.json`, and
`paper/joss-editorial-assurance.json`. A fresh panel review is required
against the revised article and generated evidence.
