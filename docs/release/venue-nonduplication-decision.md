This record prevents duplicate work while preserving future options. It is not
a manuscript, affiliation request, archive deposition, or submission.

## Journal of Statistical Software

Do not prepare a JSS article now. The repository's JOSS package already
describes the Rust-centred, polyglot software. A future JSS article is
considered only if it has a substantial, independently evaluated statistical
methods contribution that does not duplicate that software paper.

## NumFOCUS

Do not pursue NumFOCUS affiliation now. It is a sustainability affiliation,
not a peer-review route. Reassess only after demonstrated adoption and
multi-person governance make a durable community case.

## Zenodo

evidence meet current repository needs. Deposit only when a selected venue or
funder requires a DOI-bearing archive not otherwise supplied.
