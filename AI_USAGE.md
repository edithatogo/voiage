# AI-assisted development

AI-assisted development may be used for repository maintenance. Retained
changes require human review before they are represented as human-reviewed
submission material. Authorized autonomous repository delivery and automated
tests do not supply that personal attestation. Changes are checked against the
project requirements and validated by the relevant tests and workflows.
AI output is not treated as evidence of correctness or as a substitute for
authorship, peer review, or maintainer accountability.
