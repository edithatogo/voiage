# pyOpenSci and JOSS focused criteria refresh — 2026-08-27

This focused refresh records the official criteria consulted for the selected
pyOpenSci-first route. It does not authorize an inquiry or submission and does
not claim review, acceptance, publication, or indexing.

## pyOpenSci

The current pyOpenSci author guide asks the submitting maintainer to commit to
maintaining an accepted package for approximately two years. The project now
records that commitment, a best-effort issue-triage target, a non-guaranteed
fix policy, and a succession or archival boundary in `GOVERNANCE.md`,
`SUPPORT.md`, and `docs/release/pyopensci-readiness.md`.

The authenticated decision to open a pyOpenSci inquiry or review remains
human-controlled. The evidence matrix cannot perform or imply that action.

Authoritative guidance:

- <https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/how-to/author-guide.html>
- <https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/our-process/policies.html>

## JOSS partner pathway

Current pyOpenSci partnership guidance states that an accepted package may be
referred to JOSS without a second software review when the package is also in
JOSS scope. JOSS still reviews the paper, and pyOpenSci acceptance does not
guarantee JOSS acceptance. The repository therefore records the partner
pathway as selected but keeps JOSS referral, editorial review, and acceptance
as later human or external gates.

Authoritative guidance:

- <https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/partners/joss.html>
- <https://www.pyopensci.org/software-peer-review/how-to/editors-guide.html>
