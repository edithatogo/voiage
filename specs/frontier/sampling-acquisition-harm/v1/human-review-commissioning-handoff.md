# Human review commissioning handoff

This is a preparation aid for issue #870. It is not a commission, reviewer
eligibility decision, source receipt, scientific report, approval or study
authorization. The immutable H8-C packet is historical; reviewers must receive
a newly frozen replacement packet after the preconditions below are satisfied.

## Before contacting reviewers

1. Record an accountable choice among the three candidate-context options in
   `human-commissioning-preflight-20260803.json`. The recommended option is to
   seek review of the proposed exclusion of a universal automatic scalar or
   study-authorizing kernel. A generic continuation instruction is not this
   content-bound decision.
2. Obtain independent retrieval records for the governed sources, including
   representation, observation digest, drift, retention and redistribution
   rights, and jurisdiction-specific applicability. Do not infer rights from
   successful retrieval or a licence label alone.
3. Screen prospective reviewers before sending non-public packet material.
   Record a stable reviewer identifier, relevant qualifications, contribution
   and remediation history, conflicts, independence, intended role, signing
   mechanism and consent for the evidence that will be public.
4. Freeze a replacement packet only after substantive inputs are ready. Bind
   every report to its exact commit, tree and canonical packet digest.

Do not commit credentials, personal contact details, raw signatures or private
conflict narratives. Store only the minimum consented public evidence and an
authenticated receipt identifier. A maintainer may coordinate the process but
cannot self-satisfy independent scientific or domain/ethics review.

## Estimand and domain

Check the sampling action, comparator, population, affected-party set, harm
distribution, catastrophic or absorbing outcomes, commensurability assumptions,
jurisdiction and the zero-harm reduction. Report every unresolved assumption as
a finding; a positive EVSI or ENBS is not study authorization.

## Estimator assurance

Check estimator definition, truth-known fixtures, bias, Monte Carlo error,
coverage, convergence, false-safe error budget, discontinuities and uncertainty
around constraints or optima. Independence from estimator construction and
remediation is mandatory.

## Cross-language and API

Check schema and semantic consistency, numerical authority, stable versus
experimental discovery, binding parity, units, failure modes and adjacent-method
non-aliases. Repository/API observations do not replace scientific review.

## Governance and publication

Check issue, Project, roadmap, Conductor and evidence-chain consistency;
provenance, source-use constraints, dissent preservation, expiry, supersession
and the separation of scientific, maturity, release and publication decisions.

## Domain and ethics specialist

Check whether the declared domain and jurisdiction make the cited authorities
applicable, whether acquisition burdens and catastrophic harms are represented,
and whether the candidate avoids implying ethics, regulatory or real-study
authorization. This methodological role does not grant such authorization.

## H8-G confirmations

Only after H8-D through H8-F are complete, two distinct humans—one scientific
and one domain/ethics qualified—may provide candidate-bound confirmations.
Each receipt must bind the exact replacement packet, conditions, dissent,
expiry and supersession. A separate maintainer product/maturity decision then
chooses retain, implement or reviewed exclusion; it is not supplied here.

## Required outputs and verification

- Eligibility: `reviewer-attestation.schema.json`.
- Role review: `role-report.schema.json`.
- Finding decision: `disposition.schema.json`.
- Scientific confirmation: `scientific-approval.schema.json`.

Compute canonical digests using the repository evidence writer. Preserve every
finding and disagreement; do not overwrite earlier evidence. Any substantive
packet change invalidates prior reports unless a schema-valid, independently
signed bounded-metadata delta applies. H8-D remains pending until every required
role is eligible and has reviewed the same current packet.
