# Pre-submission software and method landscape refresh — 2026-08-29

This refresh extends, but does not replace, the bounded census in
`registry.json`. It records authoritative observations that must be dispositioned
in Phase 2 before any implementation or capability claim.

## Verified software observations

- The six tracked CRAN packages retain the versions recorded in the registry:
  `voi` 1.0.3, `BCEA` 2.4.83, `dampack` 1.0.2.1000, `heemod` 1.1.0,
  `hesim` 0.5.8, and `decisionSupport` 1.115.
- PyPI confirms Pyro 1.9.1, BoTorch 0.18.1, scikit-activeml 1.0.0,
  Emukit 0.5.1, and vop-poc-nz 0.1.8. `decision-security` moved to the
  security-decision-science organization and released 0.1.0.
- Two direct research implementations were absent from the prior census:
  [value-of-information](https://github.com/tadamcz/value-of-information) for
  Python and [EVSI](https://github.com/annaheath/EVSI) for R. Both are useful
  comparative evidence but are limited, old research implementations and are
  not dependency candidates.
- Public documentation identifies the MetricGate EVPI and Pharma Org HEOR
  calculators. They remain `not-reproducible`: no public, versioned source and
  numerical fixture set was verified.

## Disposition boundary

The existing 59-method census remains the canonical taxonomy for this phase.
No candidate above is promoted until Phase 2 records its estimand, assumptions,
reference algorithm, numerical fixtures, maintenance cost, and relationship to
the stable API. Hosted calculators are comparison surfaces, not dependencies or
reproducibility evidence. The search remains bounded and cannot establish
universal completeness.
