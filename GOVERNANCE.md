# Governance

`voiage` is maintained by its code owner. Pull requests and automated checks
provide the auditable change boundary; the maintainer performs the final review
and merge decision. Contributions, issue reports, and proposals are welcome
through GitHub and are assessed against the project roadmap and documented
quality and security requirements.

The maintainer commits to maintaining the package for at least two years after
any pyOpenSci acceptance. Supported-release maintenance covers reproducible
defects, security reports, packaging compatibility, and documentation needed to
use the stable public contract. Response and remediation targets are
best-effort, not guaranteed service levels; the detailed policy and succession
boundary are recorded in `docs/release/pyopensci-readiness.md`.

External review, registry acceptance, and publication decisions remain with
the relevant maintainers and editors.
